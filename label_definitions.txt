supported_take: A post that makes a basketball claim and supports it with a specific checkable fact, statistic, historical example, or explained basketball reasoning.

unsupported_take: A post that makes a basketball judgment, ranking, prediction, or confident claim without enough supporting evidence or reasoning to establish an argument.

discussion_prompt: A post that primarily asks the community to choose, rank, predict, compare, or give an opinion instead of presenting its own developed argument.

DECISION RULE:

If a post gives specific evidence or explains a basketball mechanism that directly supports its main claim, label it supported_take.

If it mainly states a ranking, prediction, judgment, or conclusion without developing why, label it unsupported_take.

A statistic, number, or player name by itself does not automatically make a post supported. The information must be used to support the main claim.

If a post asks a question but also gives an argument, determine its main purpose. If most of the post develops the writer's own claim, use supported_take or unsupported_take. If its main purpose is getting answers from other users, use discussion_prompt.
